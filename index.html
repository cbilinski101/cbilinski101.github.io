HOW I APPROACH A PROBLEM

Make it easier to find, understand and use.

I notice when a task involves too much searching, repeated entry or information that is hard to follow. I'm naturally inclined to ask how a process works, why it's done that way, and whether there's a more efficient or reliable approach.

My background in customer service, administration, bookkeeping and reporting helps me understand everyday work problems. My personal projects give me a way to turn that experience into practical tools—and keep improving them through use and testing.

I also adapt spreadsheets between Excel and Google Sheets, adjusting formulas that are not directly interchangeable and checking the results after conversion.
