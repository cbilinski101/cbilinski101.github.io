Featured personal project · In development

Volleyball Scorekeeping Helper

A courtside need became an ongoing project in making a complicated task easier to follow.

The need
Learning to keep score while following a live volleyball game meant managing lineups, rotations, substitutions and the score sheet at the same time.
My response
I started a guided, phone-friendly app to bring those tasks together. I shape its requirements around courtside use and use testing and feedback to decide what needs to improve.
What it demonstrates
Initiative, practical problem-solving, attention to the user, and persistence through repeated testing and revisions.

Currently under active development and testing. Regular and practice flows, team setup, scoring, saved history and an OVA-style score-sheet preview are included. Some device, printing and exceptional-rule paths still need verification. The official record and event officials remain the reference.
